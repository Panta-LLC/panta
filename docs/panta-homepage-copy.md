## 5. How it works

**Section header:** No mystery, no runaround.

**Step 1 — Pulse Check.**
A free 30-minute conversation about where things stand. You'll get a one-page readout within 48 hours — three observations and one recommendation, yours to keep either way.

**Step 2 — Plan together.**
If it makes sense to work together, we scope something small and clear. Fixed price, plain terms, no retainers you don't understand.

**Step 3 — Build and support.**
We do the work, show you how it works, and stay reachable. You're never locked in and never left stranded.

## Copy rules for the build

1. Never say "leverage," "solutions," "empower," "seamless," or "digital transformation."
2. CTAs are verbs. Every section that isn't the editorial strip routes to the Pulse Check.
3. "Pulse Check" is always capitalized as a proper noun; "Pulse" alone refers to the publication.
   - Buttons describe, copy brands: button text must pass the no-context test ("Book a free 30-minute review"); the Pulse Check name lives in headlines and microcopy around buttons, never on them. Revisit once the name has earned recognition (or for warm traffic arriving from Pulse articles).
4. Metrics must be real. Placeholder brackets stay until a true number exists — no invented stats.
5. Write to one reader at a time. Default reader: a community org leader with too much on her plate.
