## Executive Summary

Cedar & Stone has a visibility engine that's working and a conversion path that's quietly throwing away what that engine produces.

You're findable: you rank in the top 5 of the map pack for "landscaping [city]," your Google Business Profile is claimed and reasonably complete, and you have a healthy 4.7-star rating. An estimated **340 people a month** land on one of your digital entry points. But from there, the path collapses — your contact form fails on mobile about a third of the time, inquiries wait an average of **2.5 business days** for a reply, and there's no way to see which channels produce customers because analytics was never configured.

**The one-sentence diagnosis: You don't have a visibility problem. You have a follow-through problem — and it's the cheapest kind to fix.**

At your stated average job value of $4,200, closing even two of the leaks below conservatively represents **$8–17k/month in recoverable revenue**. Most of the fixes are small; three are things you can do yourself this month.

## Top 3 Money Leaks

### Leak #1 — The mobile contact form fails. *(est. $6–10k/month)*
71% of your site traffic is mobile. On iOS Safari, your contact form's submit button is overlapped by the cookie banner and the date-picker doesn't open — we reproduced this on three devices. Conservatively, if a third of mobile inquiries die here, that's **8–12 lost inquiries a month**. At your 25% close rate and $4,200 average job: this single bug likely costs more per month than this audit costs once.
**Fix:** [Project — small] Form replacement + testing, or [DIY] if your Squarespace theme allows swapping to the native form block.

### Leak #2 — 2.5-day response time. *(est. $3–5k/month)*
We submitted two mystery inquiries. One got a reply in 2 days, one in 3. Industry research consistently shows contractors who respond within an hour win the majority of jobs — homeowners fill out three forms and hire whoever calls back first. You're almost certainly winning only the jobs where nobody else answered either.
**Fix:** [DIY] An auto-reply that sets expectations + a phone notification for form fills gets you 80% of the value this week. [Project] A simple inquiry→SMS pipeline gets you the rest.

### Leak #3 — Your best proof is invisible. *(est. $2k+/month, compounding)*
You have 47 reviews at 4.7 stars and genuinely impressive before/after photos — trapped in your phone and on a dormant Instagram. The website shows zero reviews, zero recent projects, and a portfolio page last updated in 2024. Meanwhile the unclaimed Houzz profile — where high-budget homeowners actually shop — shows six mediocre photos you didn't choose.
**Fix:** [DIY] Claim Houzz today (free, 20 minutes). [Project] Review widget + portfolio refresh on the site.

## Findings by Channel

**Google Business Profile — 8/10 checks passing.** Claimed, verified, correct categories, 23 photos. Gaps: no Posts since March (freshness signal), and your "Request a Quote" action button links to the broken form (see Leak #1).

**Website — the weak link.** Headline reads "Welcome to Cedar & Stone" — says nothing about what you do or where (fails the 5-second test; costs you both clarity and SEO). LCP is 4.8s on mobile (failing; mostly uncompressed hero images). No schema markup. HTTPS fine, no broken links beyond the Linktree.

**Reviews — strong asset, no system.** 47 Google reviews (4.7★), 9 Yelp (4.5★), most recent 5 weeks ago. You respond to about half — good instinct, inconsistent practice. There is no systematic ask: your last 30 reviews came from customers who volunteered. A post-job text with a review link would likely double your velocity.

**Social — right platforms, wrong rhythm.** Instagram is the correct primary channel for landscaping (visual, local discovery) but has been dormant for 11 weeks. Facebook is active-ish via sporadic reposts. Neither links back to the site correctly (see map above).

**NAP consistency — one real problem.** Your Yelp listing shows the old Maple St. address from 2023. Everything else matches. Fifteen-minute fix, real local-SEO impact.
