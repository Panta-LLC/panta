# Brand update — social posts

Announcing the V1 2026 identity. Written against `BrandGuide.pdf` §07 Voice.

**Voice constraints obeyed:** plain-spoken, reassuring, specific; name the problem
then remove it. No jargon, no hype, no pressure — and **no preaching of the name's
origin**, which §07 rules out explicitly. Contact details use `panta.llc`, not the
`panta.co` printed in §08 (that domain does not resolve).

---

## Facebook

Panta looks a little different today.

Same work, same hands — a clearer way of showing it.

The new mark is three rings, thickest at the center and thinning outward: one clear act at the core, its effect widening. That isn't decoration. It's how the work actually goes — we find the one thing worth fixing first, build it properly, and let it carry.

Most small businesses and nonprofits don't need a tech team. They need someone who can think it through and then make the thing, for a bit.

If your site or your systems have been sitting on the someday list, that's a good place to begin.

One good move, felt everywhere.

hello@panta.llc · panta.llc
